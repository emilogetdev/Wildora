Our mission

Wildora helps curious explorers learn about plants, wildlife and the natural world. Use it as a starting point for discovery, then verify important observations with trusted field guides or experts.

AI photo guide

The current photo model provides general visual category suggestions. It is not a certified species-identification tool and can make mistakes. Never eat or handle an unknown plant, mushroom or animal based on an AI result.
